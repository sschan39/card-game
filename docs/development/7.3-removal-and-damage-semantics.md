# 7.3 — Destruction, sacrifice and damage semantics

Status: implemented supported semantic removal, regeneration/prohibition and generic damage results. Inspected at `60e9349`, 12 October 2026. [Development index](README.md).

## Purpose and structure

The same final zone destination can arise from different operations. Destruction restrictions must not prevent sacrifice or zero-toughness settlement.

| Module | Owns |
|---|---|
| `src/engine/effect-registry.ts` | Generic destroy/sacrifice/damage producers |
| `src/types/game-mutation.types.ts` | `DESTROY_CARD`, `SACRIFICE_CARD`, `REGENERATE_CARD`, shared damage batch |
| `src/engine/replacement-rules.ts` | Indestructible destruction prohibition, self-replacement precedence, regeneration/protection |
| `src/engine/proposed-events.ts` | Preserve semantic kind until replacement finishes |
| `src/engine/damage-keywords.ts` | Actual damage result consumers |
| `src/engine/state-based-planner.ts` | Lethal/deathtouch destruction versus direct zero-toughness movement |

Exact live incarnations are required for semantic destruction/sacrifice. Indestructible prohibits ordinary destruction; self-replacements have their defined precedence. Regeneration replaces destruction with tapping, damage clearing and removal from combat, consuming the applicable shield. Sacrifice and direct zone movement do not become destruction merely because they reach graveyard.

## Flow

```text
Semantic removal/damage instruction → matching replacements/prohibition
 → surviving transfer or regeneration/damage results
 → actual events → simultaneous SBA settlement → triggers/priority
```

Deathtouch marks actual dealt damage; lifelink uses final dealt amounts before losses settle. Infect/wither produce poison/counters. Protection prevention uses shared effective source qualities. Result replacement may change the loss/counters or move the recipient while damage's actual dealt event remains observable. Full prevention produces no dealt-damage consequences.

## Extension boundary and remaining work

Damage assignment keyword constraints remain separate deferred combat consumers. Token creation/copies, attachment actions and loyalty/battle gameplay remain 8.2–8.4. New removal kinds need a semantic proposal and actual-event contract, not a `MOVE_CARD` reason that is discarded before matching.

## Acceptance evidence

`tests/engine/removal-damage-rules.test.ts` covers indestructible/self precedence, regeneration, sacrifice, stale refs and generic result consumers. `tests/engine/m7-boundaries.test.ts` covers damage whose result redirects/moves its recipient. Socket replacement/prevention/removal scenarios live in `tests/server/live-routing.test.ts`.

## Stop condition

Stop if every graveyard move is treated as destruction, regeneration reduces damage without replacing destruction, or fully prevented damage still grants lifelink/marks deathtouch.
